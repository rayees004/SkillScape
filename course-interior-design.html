Interior Design Course + Internship

A comprehensive 6-month hands-on immersion into residential and commercial interior architecture, industry-standard CAD/3D software pipelines, materials, cost estimations, and a guaranteed real-world studio internship.

Course Overview & Who It Is For

Interior design is not just picking colors; it is the discipline of spatial harmony, ergonomic flow, construction documentation, tactile materiality, and realistic visualization. This 6-month intensive diploma bridges the gap between creative imagination and industry-ready execution.

Learners work on actual floor plans, produce municipal-grade working drawings, model custom modular furniture, render photorealistic 3D views, and prepare project BOQs (Bill of Quantities). The final phase places you directly into a supervised design studio internship.

Who This Course Is For:

  • ✓ Students and graduates looking to build a high-demand creative design career.
  • ✓ Career switchers seeking practical entry into architecture and interior studios.
  • ✓ People restarting after a career break or homemakers exploring design entrepreneurship.
  • ✓ Civil engineering or architecture diploma holders seeking software mastery.

What You Will Learn & Curriculum Highlights

Module 1: Design Fundamentals, Anthropometrics & Spatial Planning
Principles of interior design, human ergonomics, circulation flows, zone planning, moodboards, and freehand sketching conventions.
Module 2: Technical Drafting with AutoCAD (2D & Construction Details)
Precision floor plans, electrical & plumbing layouts, reflected ceiling plans (RCP), sectional elevations, and municipal working drawings.
Module 3: 3D Spatial Modeling (SketchUp & 3ds Max)
3D massing, custom cabinetry, modular kitchen design, false ceilings, polygon optimization, and import/export coordination.
Module 4: Lighting, Materials & Rendering (V-Ray, D5 Render & Coohom)
Physical sky and HDRI setups, artificial lighting fixtures (IES profiles), PBR texture maps, fabric folds, and instant 4K cloud rendering.
Module 5: Estimation, BOQ & Material Specifications
Square footage calculation, tile/paint estimation, rate analysis, contractor contracts, client presentations, and site measurement protocols.
Module 6: Studio Internship & Portfolio Capstone
Mentored industry project. Work with real client specifications to deliver a complete 3BHK turnkey interior design book and technical drawings.

Software & Tools Mastered:

AutoCAD SketchUp 3ds Max V-Ray Coohom D5 Render Mattoboard Planner 5D BOQ Spreadsheets

What You Will Be Able to Do

Upon graduating this course, you will possess verifiable technical competence and an end-to-end design portfolio:

  • ◆ Read and create full architectural floor plans, electrical layouts, and millwork shop drawings.
  • ◆ Produce commercial-quality 3D render views and VR walkthroughs for clients and builders.
  • ◆ Select materials, finishes, lighting fixtures, and calculate precise budget BOQs.
  • ◆ Present designs confidently in design review meetings or client pitches.
* Note: SKILLSCAPE provides practical industry-standard skill training and portfolio preparation. We do not make misleading guaranteed-placement claims; your career progression depends on your skills, portfolio rigor, and professional interviews.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need prior architecture or drawing background? ▾
No. The course begins with spatial planning principles and computer-aided drafting from the ground up. Beginners, career switchers, and graduates from any stream are welcome.
How does the internship phase work? ▾
In months 5 and 6, students participate in real studio project work under practicing architect mentors, delivering actual residential/commercial project deliverables.
Are flexible payment and installment options available? ▾
Yes, flexible installment options are available across the program duration. Connect with our admissions counselors on WhatsApp for cohort details.
