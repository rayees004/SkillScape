Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with SKILLSCAPE via our course enquiry form, brochure request form, or WhatsApp chat, we collect minimal necessary details including your Name, Email Address, Phone/WhatsApp number, and course areas of interest. We do not collect unnecessary sensitive personal data.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use this information exclusively to:

  • Provide requested course brochures, fee schedules, and syllabus breakdowns.
  • Coordinate course admissions, schedule counseling sessions, and confirm workshop registrations.
  • Send administrative updates regarding batch timings or academic calendar adjustments via WhatsApp or Email.

3. No Third-Party Selling

SKILLSCAPE does not sell, rent, or lease learner contact information to third-party telemarketers or advertisers. Your data remains strictly confidential within our academic admissions team.

4. Student Work & Testimonials Consent

In adherence to our policy, student names, project screenshots, and testimonials are published only with prior explicit permission from the learner.

5. Contacting Our Data Desk

For questions or to request removal of your contact details from our records, contact us directly at skillscapelbg@gmail.com or call +91 62386 83077.
