Financial Clarity

Fee & Refund Policy

Effective Date: October 24, 2026

1. Transparent Pricing Philosophy

SKILLSCAPE believes that quality skill training should be within everyone’s reach at honest prices. All course fees listed on our website and brochures are inclusive of instructor mentorship, lab workstation access, and course deliverables with no hidden charges.

2. Seat Reservation & Batch Allocation

Due to physical workstation limits and restricted cohort sizes (to ensure personal mentor attention), a registration deposit is required to confirm seat allocation in upcoming batches.

3. Withdrawal & Cancellation Timeline

  • 7+ Days Before Batch Start: 100% refund of course fees paid (less nominal banking transaction charges).
  • Within 3 Days Before Batch Start: 80% refund or free batch transfer to a future scheduled cohort.
  • After Batch Commencement (Orientation): Refunds are evaluated on a case-by-case basis before the conclusion of week 1. Thereafter, fees paid are non-refundable, but learners may transfer to a subsequent cohort with medical/compassionate justification.

4. Installment Payment Plans

For longer programs (e.g. 6-Month Interior Design Course + Internship or 6-Month Cybersecurity), monthly installment plans are available. Students must clear installments per agreed milestone dates to maintain uninterrupted access to cloud labs and internship placement.

5. Processing Refunds

Approved refunds are processed via the original payment mode (UPI, Net Banking, or Bank Transfer) within 5 to 7 business days of written application to skillscapelbg@gmail.com.
