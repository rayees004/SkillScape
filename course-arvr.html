Unreal Engine 5 • Unity • D5 Render

AI-Powered AR/VR

Build interactive spatial 3D environments, virtual architectural walkthroughs, and augmented reality interactions using Unreal Engine 5, Unity, and D5 Render augmented by AI generative asset pipelines.

Overview & Who It Is For

Static 3D images are giving way to real-time interactive worlds. Whether for architectural virtual walk-throughs on Meta Quest, industrial digital twins, simulation training, or interactive showrooms, real-time engines are transforming how physical space is experienced before it is built.

In 20 intensive days, you will learn how to bring CAD and 3D architectural models into Unreal Engine 5 and Unity, apply Lumen dynamic lighting, construct VR interaction blueprints, render cinematic video sequences in D5 Render, and leverage AI models for seamless procedural textures and environment concepts.

Who This Course Is For:

  • ✓ 3D visualizers and animators wanting to transition to real-time engine workflows.
  • ✓ Architects and interior designers aiming to showcase interactive VR spaces.
  • ✓ Multimedia and game design enthusiasts seeking practical studio skills.

Curriculum & Practical Modules

Module 1: Real-Time Engine Fundamentals & Asset Ingestion
Datasmith workflows from Revit, SketchUp, and 3ds Max into Unreal Engine 5. Nanite geometry virtualization and mesh optimization.
Module 2: Lighting, Materials & Generative AI Texturing
Lumen dynamic global illumination, reflection captures, post-processing color grading, and AI-assisted PBR texture synthesis.
Module 3: VR Interaction Blueprints & Spatial Navigation
Setting up OpenXR pawn, teleportation mechanics, physical door opening, material switching widgets, and interactive object inspection.
Module 4: Real-Time Architectural Cinematics with D5 Render
Atmospheric weather simulation, vegetation scattering, keyframe camera paths, 60fps 4K video exports, and client VR presentation packaging.

Production Software:

Unreal Engine 5 Unity 3D D5 Render Datasmith Meta Quest VR SDK Generative AI Tools

What You Will Be Able to Do

  • ◆ Convert architectural models into playable VR walkthrough experiences.
  • ◆ Build custom material and furniture configurator menus using visual blueprints.
  • ◆ Render cinematic 4K video reels with animated lighting and natural weather effects.
* Note: SKILLSCAPE provides practical hands-on software mastery and portfolio creation. Career placement depends on student interview results and personal dedication.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a high-end gaming PC to learn? ▾
For classroom/offline studio batches, SKILLSCAPE provides dedicated high-performance RTX GPU workstations. For online learners, a modern dedicated GPU (RTX 3060 or higher recommended) is ideal.
Is C++ programming required for Unreal Engine? ▾
No. We focus on Unreal Engine’s visual Blueprint scripting system, which requires zero traditional line-by-line code and is the industry choice for architectural visualization and VR interaction.
